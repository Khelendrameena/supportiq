# Acme Cloud Support Handbook

## Refund policy
We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee on all paid plans. To request a refund,
email billing@acme.example with your invoice number. Refunds are processed
within 5 business days.

## Password reset
Go to Settings, then Security, then click Reset Password. You will receive an
email with a reset link valid for 1 hour.

## Support hours
Our support team is available Monday to Friday, 9am to 6pm IST. Emergency
support for enterprise plans is available 24/7.

## Data export
You can export all your data from Settings, then Data, then Export. Exports
are delivered as a ZIP file within 24 hours.
