# Acme Cloud Billing FAQ

## Accepted payment methods
We accept Visa, Mastercard, and bank transfers for annual plans. Monthly plans
are card-only.

## Invoice copies
Download invoice copies anytime from Billing, then Invoices. Invoices are kept
for 7 years.

## Plan upgrades
Upgrading mid-cycle applies prorated credit automatically. Downgrades take
effect at the next billing date and are not prorated.

## Failed payments
If a payment fails, we retry after 3 days and again after 7 days. Your service
is suspended only after the second failed retry.
